Stagehands

Mr Box, Episode 2. Pio is inside the box. Listen for these lines. The bold line is when you move.

  1. Before

    [Box comes out of the door, singing]

    I’m a little box, in a box world, life in cardboard, it’s fantastic…

    Box waits behind the door with the microphone inside. Staircase clear. Fire staff ready off stage, wet towel next to it.

  2. Forklift

    O-oh — staircase.

    But this time we are prepared, is that right safety? Forklift please. Foooorkliiift

    Oww, no forklift. We boxes like forklifts.

    Shrug, and shake head. Nobody comes.

  3. Carry the box down

    But I like if you carry me too. Just be gentle.

    I might not be insured up to the value of contents.

    … going down …

    Uuu, this feels so good. Soft touch, golden heart.

    Are you married?

    TAA-Daa

    Come up and carry the box down the stairs. Slowly, gently. Put it down at the bottom on “TAA-Daa” and step away.

  4. Box talks to the crowd

    Okeeey, Hell yeah, fire circus!

    … a few minutes of talking …

    When I was a little box I wanted to be a pizza box

    But you’re probably like — is he fireproof and all?

    Stay off stage. On “fireproof”, light the staff and wait at the edge.

  5. Bring the fire

    Do you guys think that a little box like me can spin fire in a circus on a stage?

    [Crowd — Yeaaaaa]

    Safety, you know what to do — take back the microphone and bring me the fire staff!

    Take the microphone from the box. Bring the lit fire staff and put it in front of the box.

  6. After

    When the box drops the staff, safety takes it and puts it out.
