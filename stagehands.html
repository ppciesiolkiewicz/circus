Stagehands

What to do around the stage while Pio plays Disguise.

  1. Before

    Mic stand in the middle. Guitar, looper and cables ready. Tape the cables down — people walk here, and there is fire.

  2. Looper

    Pio might need it moved during the song. Stay close, move it only when he points.

  3. Fire staff

    Prepared and waiting off stage, with a wet towel next to it. Do not light it until Pio gives the sign.

  4. During

    Stay off the stage. Never walk between Pio and the looper. Keep the front edge clear.

  5. After

    When he sings “open stage starts soon”, the song is ending. Take guitar and looper off, leave the mic for open stage.
