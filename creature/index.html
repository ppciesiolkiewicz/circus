Things to keep in mind

    Cast

    Filled dot = high shape (back lines). Hollow ring = low shape (front lines). Dashed outer ring = responsible. Pio starts at front centre, dies and holds downstage centre while the lines pass behind. After 4 bars of running (adjustable) Pio joins in, crossing edge to edge on a line in front of everyone, in step with the smaller side (the first time they pass mid-stage), then joins the closing line. The song start time marks the run-in, so Pio’s walk plays just before it. Each side's single file splits into 2 lines: a high line upstage and a low line downstage, interleaved with the other side so the groups pass each other. One edge-to-edge crossing takes 8 beats (adjustable); everyone moves fastest at the edges and slowest through the middle.
